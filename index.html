What it does

Everything you do with your screen, in one place.

OneFrame replaces the screenshot shortcut, a markup app, an OCR tool and a screen recorder.

All‑in‑One

One shortcut opens a frame you can drag, resize to exact pixels and snap to window edges. Then choose screenshot, scroll, text or video.

Fast markup

Arrows, shapes, text, counters, highlighter, blur and pixelate. Add a gradient or image background and export.

Screen recording

MP4 with system audio, microphone and camera bubble. Draw on screen, highlight clicks and show the keys you press.

Scrolling capture

Scroll down or up and OneFrame stitches the page into one long image.

Text recognition

Select any area and get the text on your clipboard. Works offline, in many languages.

Pin & share

Pin a shot above other windows at 1:1, drag it into any app, or upload and copy a link that expires on its own.

FAQ

Questions

Opening the beta
  1. Drag OneFrame to Applications.
  2. If macOS says it can’t verify the developer: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  3. If it says the app is damaged, run in Terminal:
    xattr -cr /Applications/OneFrame.app
  4. Allow Screen Recording when asked, then reopen OneFrame.
Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 13 Ventura or later, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Does it replace the built‑in ⇧⌘3 / ⇧⌘4?

Yes. OneFrame takes over the same shortcuts and shows you how to turn the system ones off in one step.

Where do my screenshots go?

To a folder you choose (Pictures/OneFrame by default) and to the clipboard. Nothing leaves your Mac unless you press Upload.

How long do share links live?

You choose 1 day, 3 days or 1 week. After that the file is deleted automatically.

Is there a subscription?

No. Pro is a single purchase.
